 TENS QUESTÕES? NÓS TEMOS RESPOSTAS. 
 
Perguntas Frequentes
 What is a term deposit? 
 A term deposit is a type of savings account where you deposit money with a bank for a set period of time. You receive interest on this deposit, which is calculated using a fixed rate. It’s a safe and predictable option for those who want to grow their savings. 
 How long can I leave my money in a term deposit? 
 The term of a term deposit can vary. The timeframe you choose should be based on your financial objectives and how quickly you need access to your money. This condition is defined in the Standardised Information Sheet (FIN in Portuguese) of each term deposit, along with the conditions for redeeming the deposit early. 
 How does the return for a term deposit work? 
 The return on term deposits is guaranteed and fixed from the outset. You will know exactly how much your money will have earned by the end of the agreed term, which gives you a sense of predictability and security. 
 Can I redeem my money before the deadline? 
 Although the purpose of a term deposit is to hold the money until the end of the term, early redemption is usually allowed. Depending on the conditions set, however, there may be penalties or loss of interest. Some term deposits do not allow early redemption. You should consult the standardised information sheet to find out this information before setting up the term deposit. 
 Is my deposit money safe? 
 Term deposits are guaranteed up to a maximum of €100,000, as they are protected by the Deposit Guarantee Fund.
